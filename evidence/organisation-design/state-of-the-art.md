# State of the art

Step 3 of the review. For each question, the best evidence, kept apart from the practice most adopted. Upper bounds (what was achieved) are kept apart from limits (what was shown not to work). Sources are in [review](review.md).

**Shape:** [state of the art](https://github.com/Tewf/reader-ergonomics-review/blob/39d3b25cd822a20277ee39c37b9e0130439802f2/shapes/state-of-the-art.md)

## Q1 What must a company do, as a list?

| Record | Holder | What it says | Grade |
|---|---|---|---|
| Best evidence | Malone 1999 | processes organised at several levels of abstraction, by inheritance and by the dependencies between activities | convention |
| Upper bound | MIT 2003 Handbook | one library of over 5000 business activities | convention |
| Limit | none found | no list tested for completeness against a theory of the firm | not found |
| Most adopted | Cragg 2007 | the cross-industry process classification, made into a 24-item instrument for 66 small firms | measured, descriptive |
| Best evidence, the legal need | Hansmann 2000 | the firm's essential legal function is asset partitioning, a person distinct from its owners | convention |

## Q2 What problems must its human architecture solve, and who decides?

| Record | Holder | What it says | Grade |
|---|---|---|---|
| Best evidence | Puranam 2014 | four universal problems, task division, task allocation, reward and information, claimed necessary and sufficient | convention |
| Upper bound | McElheran 2014 | delegation follows the value of adaptation against coordination, as team theory predicts | measured |
| Limit | Doty 1993 | Mintzberg's five ideal types did not predict effectiveness | measured |
| Most adopted | Mintzberg 1979 | five parts and five configurations of structure | convention |

## Q3 How is an organisation written as an information-processing system?

| Record | Holder | What it says | Grade |
|---|---|---|---|
| Best evidence | Radner 1993 | an organisation of limited processors, efficient by the number of processors and the delay | measured, as a model |
| Upper bound | Levitt 1999 | a simulation of a project's information processing that predicts its performance | supported with caveat |
| Limit | Van Zandt 1999 | in real time, decentralisation does not unambiguously reduce delay | measured, as a model |
| Most adopted | OMG 2013 BPMN | a process notation with a machine-readable metamodel | convention |

## Q4 How does an organisation adapt, and change its design?

| Record | Holder | What it says | Grade |
|---|---|---|---|
| Best evidence | March 1991 | adaptive processes refine exploitation faster than exploration, and so destroy themselves in the long run | measured, a simulation |
| Upper bound | Siggelkow 2005 | a sequence of structures reaches higher performance than any fixed structure | measured, a simulation |
| Limit | Levinthal 1997 | on a rugged landscape the form at founding persists | measured, a simulation |
| Most adopted | Teece 1997 | dynamic capabilities: distinctive processes, positions and paths | supported with caveat |
