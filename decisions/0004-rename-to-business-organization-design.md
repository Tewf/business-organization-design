# 0004: Rename the repository business-organization-design

**Decision:** The repository is named business-organization-design, after the field it reviews, replacing the name
record 0002 kept.

**Status:** accepted, 2026-11-12. **Shape:** [decision record](https://github.com/Tewf/reader-ergonomics/blob/65e42472e2d4cbfd994c2a86e95a47e5dc7301ca/shapes/decision-record.md).

## Context

- Record 0002 kept the name business-organisation after the frame step: "organisation" names the object that both the
  economics of the firm and organisation theory study.
- The field has two names for its two halves, design on one side and the firm's economics on the other; record 0002
  lists them, from the [frame](../evidence/organisation-design/frame.md).
- The work since the review is on the design side: B1 is a table of processes, roles and decision rights, and the
  first shape built on it describes one organisation's design ([record 0003](0003-describe-an-organisation-as-b1s-table.md)).
- The maintainer was asked on a decision page whether to keep the name, and chose to rename (12 Nov. 2026).

## Decision

- The repository is business-organization-design, on GitHub and on disk; its site is
  tewf.github.io/business-organization-design.
- The field's spelling, "organization design", is taken for the name; the text keeps its British spelling.
- Record 0002 keeps its baseline; only its name part is superseded by this record.

## Options not taken

- Keep business-organisation (the recommendation on the decision page): no link to move, but the name does not say
  which side of the field the work is on.

## Consequences

- GitHub redirects the old addresses of the repository; the site's old address does not redirect.
- The hub and every repository that links this one are updated on the same day.
- **Bad:** the name and the text spell the word two ways.
- **Bad:** the theory of the firm, the other half of the field, is no longer named by the repository; it stays in
  scope as the frame says.
