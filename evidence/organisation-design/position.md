# Position

Step 4 of the review. What is known, what was not found, and what this repository asks. Each "not found" is a statement about these searches, not about the world. Terms are in [frame](frame.md).

**Shape:** [position](https://github.com/Tewf/reader-ergonomics-review/blob/39d3b25cd822a20277ee39c37b9e0130439802f2/shapes/position.md)

## Known

- A firm's boundary is set by the cost of its transactions inside against across markets (Coase 1937, Williamson 1985).
- The law makes a firm a person that holds assets apart from its owners (Hansmann 2000, Hansmann 2006).
- What a firm does can be listed as processes at several levels of abstraction, and practice keeps such lists (Porter 1985, Malone 1999, Cragg 2007).
- Every organisation must divide tasks, allocate them, reward and inform those who do them (Puranam 2014, Martela 2019).
- Ownership, control and decision rights are separable, and delegation follows the value of local adaptation (Fama 1983, Jensen 1976, McElheran 2014).
- An organisation can be modelled as limited processors of information, and its efficiency measured by their number and by the delay (Galbraith 1974, Radner 1993, Garicano 2000).
- Adaptation drifts towards exploitation, and the starting form persists on a rugged landscape (March 1991, Levinthal 1997).

## Not found in these searches

- A list of what every firm must do, derived from theory and tested for completeness (strings F5, F7, F44, F45).
- A description of one firm that joins its processes, its four problems and its decision rights in one document a program reads (strings F9, F36, F38).
- A test of a structure designed for a young firm against one that grew without design (strings F35, F25).
- A rule for when a young firm should change its design, tested on firms (strings F25, F27, F33).

## What this repository asks

- Can one document describe a company as its processes, with the four problems answered and the decision rights named for each, so that a program counts what is missing?
- Does such a description of a young venture find gaps that its org chart hides?

## What the shapes must decide

- The list: which process classification gives the rows, read at its source by the maintainer, since its page refused an automated reader.
- The columns: the four universal problems, and who decides and who controls, for each row.
- The form: a table a program reads, so that a missing cell is counted by a check, not noticed by a reader.
- Dependencies between processes and the measures of delay are left for later; they are what B1 lacks.
