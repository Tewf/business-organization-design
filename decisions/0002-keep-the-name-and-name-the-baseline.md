# 0002: Keep the name, and compare a description of a company with B1

**Decision:** The repository keeps its name after the frame step. The baseline of its first review is B1, a process list with the four universal problems of organizing answered for each process, compared with an org chart.

**Status:** accepted, 2026-11-11. **Shape:** [decision record](https://github.com/Tewf/reader-ergonomics/blob/65e42472e2d4cbfd994c2a86e95a47e5dc7301ca/shapes/decision-record.md).

## Context

- The frame step found the field's names: organization design and organization theory on one side, the theory of the firm and organizational economics on the other ([frame](../evidence/organisation-design/frame.md)).
- The review found a practice list of processes, four problems every organisation must solve, and decision rights that are separable. It found no list tested for completeness, and no description that joins the three for one firm ([position](../evidence/organisation-design/position.md)).
- Mintzberg's five ideal types, the best-known description of structure, did not predict effectiveness in a formal test ([against](../evidence/organisation-design/against.md)).
- The review is finished only when it names a baseline.

## Decision

- Keep the name. The field splits the subject between economics and organisation theory, and "organisation" names the object both study; the subtitle restricts it to the firm.
- Name the baseline B1 ([baseline](../evidence/organisation-design/baseline.md)): rows from the cross-industry process classification plus the legal person, columns for the four problems and for who decides and who controls.
- Compare it with an org chart of the same venture, by coverage, completeness and the empty cells it finds.

## Options not taken

- A configuration as the baseline, Mintzberg's five types: they are the most adopted description, and they failed the one formal test found.
- An information-processing model as the baseline, after Radner: it measures efficiency, but no description of a real firm in its terms was found to compare with.

## Consequences

- A later description of a company, by a person or a program, can be compared with B1 cell by cell.
- **Bad:** the rows rest on a classification whose categories were not read at their source; the maintainer reads them before the shape is written.
- **Bad:** B1 has no dependencies between processes, no measure of delay and no rule for changing the design; the review names each as a gap.
- **Bad:** 38 of 53 formal sources were read from their abstracts and 7 located only, so many figures are secondary.
